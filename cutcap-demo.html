CutCap

A caption editor for Premiere Pro. Import an SRT, refine the cut and the wording right in the panel, then push the result back to your timeline.

Editing captions

  • Double-click a word to edit just that word.
  • Click a “/” between words to split the line there; the trailing “/” merges with the next line.
  • Cleanup bar — Remove gaps, Remove line breaks, Remove punctuation, all undoable.

Review & search

  • Flag for review — mark a line and jot a note.
  • Find & Replace — matches highlight as you type; Aa case, W whole-word, Replace all.

Playback & sync

  • Sync highlights the caption under the playhead as your Premiere timeline plays.

History & output

  • History reopens your recent edits — work is auto-saved.
  • Export SRT saves to disk; Push to Premiere places the captions on a new caption track.
  • Panel size lives in Settings → UI; the dark/light theme and accent in Settings → Appearance.
