CutCap

A caption editor for Premiere Pro. Import an SRT, refine the cut and the wording right in the panel, then push the result back to your timeline.

Getting started

  • Click Import, or drag a .srt, .vtt, or .csv file onto the panel (a CSV with frame timecodes asks for your frame rate once).
  • Already imported into Premiere? Drag the SRT from the Project panel onto CutCap.
  • Captions appear as editable lines, each with its timecode.

Editing captions

  • Single-click a line — moves Premiere's playhead to that caption.
  • ↑ / ↓ — walk the selection through the captions (the playhead follows); Enter edits the selected line; F flags it for review.
  • Double-click a word — edit just that word. Press Space while editing to split the line into two captions at the caret; Enter commits, Esc cancels.
  • Double-click the empty area of a line — edit the whole line as one field.
  • Click a “/” between words — split the line there. The trailing “/” merges a line with the next one.

Review & search

  • Flag for review — click the flag icon on a caption to mark it; a note field appears where you can jot why. Use the flag button in the toolbar to jump between flagged captions.
  • Find & Replace (magnifier, or Ctrl+F) — matches are highlighted as you type; Enter / Shift+Enter step through them, or Replace all. Toggle Aa for case and W for whole-word (so “at” won’t match inside “procrastinating”). Flags and notes are CutCap-only and never touch the exported SRT.

Playback & sync

  • Play / Stop button or the Spacebar toggles timeline playback.
  • Sync highlights the caption under the playhead as the sequence plays.

History & output

  • History reopens your recent edits — work is auto-saved. On launch CutCap can reopen your previous session, start fresh, or ask (set it in ☰ → Settings → On launch).
  • Undo / Redo (or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y) adjust your edits. Panel size lives in Settings → UI → UI size.
  • Export SRT saves the edited file to disk (you choose where); Push to Premiere places the captions on a new caption track and files a versioned copy (named after your sequence, e.g. Sequence_v2.srt) in a CutCap bin in the Project panel. The SRT is saved to disk in a CutCap folder beside your project file — or set your own folder in ☰ → Settings → Caption save location.

Push as MOGRTs

  • The MOGRT button (next to Push) opens a template gallery and places every caption as a Motion Graphics Template clip on a fresh video track, at the caption's exact timing — fully styleable in Premiere's Essential Graphics panel afterwards.
  • Add your templates with + (any After Effects–made .mogrt with a text field; Premiere-made ones can't be text-driven). Added templates are copied into your library at Documents\CutCap\MOGRTs.
  • When you add a template, CutCap renders a hover preview automatically (Premiere blocks for a few seconds); the Render previews button fills in previews for older templates. Previews play on hover — or loop always, via the toggle. You can still drop your own video with the same name next to a .mogrt instead.
  • Placing is one clip per caption, so a long list takes a moment — the progress bar counts along, and closing it stops the batch. Note: undoing a push is also one Ctrl+Z per clip.
